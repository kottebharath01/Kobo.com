Frequently Asked Questions

› What is BookHub?
BookHub is a digital reading platform offering eBooks and audiobooks across genres and languages, available on the web, mobile apps, and dedicated eReaders.
› What makes BookHub different?
BookHub focuses on a distraction-free reading experience, seamless sync across devices, and a large catalogue of regional-language titles alongside global bestsellers.
› What eBook formats does BookHub support?
BookHub natively supports EPUB, EPUB3, PDF, MOBI, JPEG, GIF, PNG, BMP, TIFF, TXT, HTML, RTF, CBZ and CBR files.
› Where can I buy BookHub-compatible eBooks?
The easiest way to buy a book compatible with your BookHub Reader is to purchase directly from the BookHub store. Once you have created a BookHub account, you can sign in and browse the storefront from any device, including the Reader itself. If you'd like to buy books from a third party and read them on your BookHub Reader, make sure the book is in a compatible format and free of DRM (Digital Rights Management).
